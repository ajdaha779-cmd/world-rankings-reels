Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

World in Numbers Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the World in Numbers channels (YouTube @WorldNumbers1, Instagram @ajdaha.win). It has no other users.

What the tool does

  • Uploads videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads public comments under the owner's own videos to find which country a viewer named, so the viewer's public username can be shown on the world map in a following video, and may reply to that comment.

Data we use

Only public information that viewers themselves posted under our videos: the public username and the comment text. We do not collect emails, passwords, private messages or any other personal data, and we do not sell or share data. Access tokens of the owner's account are stored as encrypted secrets and used only for the actions above.

Data retention and deletion

Public usernames shown on the map are kept only as long as the related comment exists. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days. To request deletion of your data at any time, send us a direct message on Instagram @ajdaha.win — we delete it within 7 days.

Your choices

If you do not want your username shown on our map, delete your comment or write to us and we will remove it.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at Google security settings.
